# Loyalty — YouTube Shorts Ad (20.0 s)

**Q: "Who am I? What should I know about you?" — A: "Loyalty"**

- **Platform:** YouTube Shorts · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · H.264 + AAC · −14 LUFS, −1.4 dBTP
- **Runtime:** exactly 20.03 s
- **Style:** punchy modern SaaS marketing — dark indigo art direction with violet→fuchsia→amber
  gradient accents, kinetic uppercase typography, quick hard cuts with 2-frame flashes,
  slow background push-ins, upbeat 120 BPM synth bed under a brisk voiceover (~160 wpm).
- **Audience:** small-business consumers and business decision-makers.

## Production notes

- Backgrounds/product renders: AI-generated plates (glow orbs, phone, stars); all UI
  (loyalty card, QR, chips, notification, chart, logo) drawn programmatically for crispness
  and composited with a calibrated 4-point perspective warp onto the phone screen.
- Text animation: rise-and-fade pops synced to the voiceover; scenes cut at the midpoint
  of VO gaps with 78%-white 2-frame flashes on the hard beats.
- Audio: AI voiceover (advertising voice), synthesized 120 BPM bed (Am–F–C–G pads, bass
  plucks, kick/hats/snare, final impact hit at the CTA logo), mixed and normalized to
  YouTube's −14 LUFS target with −1.4 dB true peak.
